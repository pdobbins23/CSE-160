Please use a browser that supports "canvas"

Blocky World

Click the canvas to capture the mouse for look. Press Esc to release.

Movement

  • W / S — forward / back
  • A / D — strafe left / right
  • Q / E — pan left / right
  • Space / Shift — up / down
  • Mouse — look around

Building

  • Left click — place block in front of you
  • Right click — remove block in front of you
  • 1–4 — select texture for placed block
